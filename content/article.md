# How AI Could Change a Small Bookshop

Maya runs a small bookshop in a busy city. Every week, she spends hours answering similar questions about opening times, book availability, and delivery. She decides to try an AI assistant for these routine questions.

At first, Maya is worried that customers will dislike talking to a machine. However, she discovers that many people appreciate quick answers when the shop is closed. The assistant cannot recommend every book well, so Maya still talks directly with customers who want personal advice.

After a month, Maya spends less time answering repeated questions and more time organizing events for local readers. She also notices a problem: the assistant occasionally gives incorrect information about stock. To solve this, she checks the answers regularly and clearly tells customers when they are speaking to an AI.

Maya believes that technology is useful when it supports people rather than replacing every human interaction. She now asks a larger question: how can small businesses use AI responsibly without losing the personal relationships that make them special?
